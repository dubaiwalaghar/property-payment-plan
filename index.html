Other costs that can sometimes apply
  • Real estate agency commission — typically 2% of price + 5% VAT, if applicable to the deal
  • Developer NOC fee (resale properties only) — roughly AED 500–5,000, varies by developer
  • Mortgage arrangement & property valuation fees — only if you're financing, roughly 1% of loan + AED 2,500–3,500
  • Service charges — an annual per-sqft maintenance fee set by the developer, starts after handover
DLD fee, admin fee and title deed fee are standard Dubai Land Department charges and are fairly fixed. Everything above is indicative — always confirm exact figures with the developer or DLD before transferring funds.
